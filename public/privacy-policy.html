Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PrepMaster AI is committed to protecting your personal information and student privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our data practices across our web application and WhatsApp integration.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Your phone number, name, and email address used for sign-in and session verification.
  • Academic Profile: Your target examination (JAMB, WAEC, NECO), target course, subjects, confidence levels, and scheduled exam dates.
  • Learning & Performance Telemetry: Question attempt histories, accuracy metrics, identified weak topics, and spaced repetition intervals needed to personalize your study recommendations.
  • Payment Details: Transaction identifiers and payment status provided by our payment gateway (Bachs). We never process or store raw credit/debit card numbers directly.

2. Purpose of Processing

Your data is processed strictly to tailor your study plan, grade practice drills, deliver timed CBT mock results, activate passes, calculate referral commissions, and provide step-by-step academic explanations. We do not sell or monetize personal student data to external third-party advertisers.

3. Third-Party Integrations

To deliver our services, we coordinate with trusted infrastructure partners:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business API): To deliver low-data messaging and practice interactions on mobile.
  • Bachs: For secure checkout and commission disbursements.
  • ALOC Exam API: For retrieving authentic Nigerian curriculum past questions.
  • AI Model Gateways (Gemini / Anthropic / Groq / OpenRouter): To generate step-by-step solutions without using your personal information for public model training.

4. Data Retention & Security

We employ industry-standard encryption, tokenized cookies, and secure server-side controls to safeguard your study history. You may request account deactivation and data removal at any time through our contact channels.
